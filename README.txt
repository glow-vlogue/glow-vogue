# Glow & Vogue — Fashion & Beauty Website

This is the first version of the website.

## Before publishing
1. Replace every occurrence of `254700000000` in `index.html` with the WhatsApp number in international format, without `+` or spaces.
   Example: a Kenyan number `0712 345 678` becomes `254712345678`.
2. Replace "Glow & Vogue" with your final business name.
3. Replace the sample services/prices with your actual offers.

## WhatsApp
The site already has WhatsApp buttons. Once the number is replaced, customers can tap them and WhatsApp will open with a pre-filled message.
